## 4. Visual Reference Standards for the 3D Reconstruction
From careful examination of the reference photographs and frames, the Three.js 3D model architecture incorporates:
1. **Entrance Architecture:** Pylon-flanked gateway with security gatehouses and upper horizontal lintel beam.
2. **Central Circulatory System:** Two-lane entrance roadway leading to a central circular traffic island / roundabout with manicured grass and ornamental plantings.
3. **Main Administrative Complex:** 3-story concrete structure finished in warm off-white/cream tones with terracotta brick accents, central glass curtain wall facade, and drive-through entrance portico canopy.
4. **Terrace Roof Features:** Parapet perimeter walls and triangular steel/glass gazebo canopy structure on the central roof.
5. **Academic Wings (CSE, IT, Mechanical, Mining, Civil):** Symmetrical 3-level rectilinear blocks with recessed window banks, exterior sunshade projections (chajjas), and connecting corridors.
6. **Workshop & Laboratories Complex:** Low-rise industrial workshop structures with blue pitched/gabled roofs.
7. **Hostels & Amenities:** Perimeter accommodation blocks, canteen facility, and sports ground.
