DIGITAL TWIN 2.0 ARCHITECTURE

A Grounded, Truthful Campus Intelligence Layer

The RCERT Smart Campus Digital Twin is engineered to provide genuine operational insight without requiring physical hardware sensors or video surveillance. It integrates public meteorological forecasts, atmospheric air-quality projections, conceptual spatial twins, and persistent incident tracking.

Unlike traditional prototype dashboards that invent artificial energy or occupancy numbers, this system enforces rigorous data provenance: every single number displayed carries its source, classification, timestamp, and confidence state.

STRICT DATA PROVENANCE

Data Classification Standard

  • Weather Intelligence: Live Open-Meteo model forecasts for campus lookup point (19.9501° N, 79.2993° E).
  • Air Quality Intelligence: CAMS global atmospheric model estimates. No physical street sensors represented.
  • Campus Spatial Twin: Conceptual Three.js geometry based on institutional zones; not an architectural survey.
  • Hardware Independence: No campus meters, ESP32 boards, or physical sensors required for live operation.
  • CCTV Status: Camera analytics and YOLO computer vision are deferred to Phase 2 (clearly documented).
  • Historical Datasets: Synthetic legacy files preserved strictly for academic reproducibility.
