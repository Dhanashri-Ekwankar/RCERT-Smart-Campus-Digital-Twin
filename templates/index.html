FUTURE DEVELOPMENT ROADMAP

Phase 2: CCTV & Computer Vision Architecture

📹
Intentional Architectural Deferral

To ensure 100% hardware-independent reliability for academic demonstration, computer vision and CCTV camera streaming are scheduled for physical deployment in Phase 2.

Planned Phase 2 Specifications:

  • Hardware Requirements: Authorized ONVIF/RTSP IP cameras connected across campus gates and corridors.
  • Local Edge Inference: Ultralytics YOLO11n ONNX runtime processing transient JPEG streams on local GPU hardware.
  • Anonymous Tripwire Tracking: Virtual entry/exit lines measuring directional pedestrian movement.
  • Privacy-First Protocol: Zero facial recognition, zero biometric logging, and zero permanent video storage.
  • Graceful Fallback: Digital Twin continues operating smoothly even when physical cameras are powered off.
Phase 1 is complete and operates independently of physical camera hardware.
